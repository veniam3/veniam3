### ♡ 01 — ABOUT ME ♡

*Curiosity is the foundation of every great creation.*

</div>

Hello! I'm **Veniam**, a 14-year-old programmer interested in computer science, systems engineering and creative technologies.

My main focus is **systems programming and Rust development**. I enjoy exploring how computers work beneath the surface: memory management, operating system internals, low-level software, and the infrastructure that keeps applications running.

I like understanding the mechanisms behind the tools I use instead of treating them as black boxes.

Outside programming, I'm also a **domra player, artist and sculptor**. I enjoy combining technical thinking with creativity, both in digital projects and in traditional art.
